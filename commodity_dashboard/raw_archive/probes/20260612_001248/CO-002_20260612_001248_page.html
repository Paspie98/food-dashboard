Cocoa Daily Prices

  • The ICCO daily price for cocoa beans is the average of the quotations of the nearest three active futures trading months on ICE Futures Europe (London) and ICE Futures US (New York) at the time of London close.
  • London prices are converted into United States dollars per tonne by using the current six-month forward rate of exchange in London at closing.
  • The time for shift to the next three-month period is the fifteenth of the month preceding the nearest active maturing month.
  • More detailed information is available in the Quarterly Bulletin of Cocoa Statistics published by ICCO.

Statistical Information and Publications – Access Update

The ICCO’s statistical information and regular market publications are a core component of the Organization’s mandate to monitor, analyse and disseminate reliable data on the global cocoa economy.

In accordance with its mandate, the ICCO remains a non-profit intergovernmental organization. At the same time, and in line with practices adopted by other international commodity organizations, the ICCO is adjusting access to its statistical information and publications as part of its efforts to support the financial sustainability of its statistical and analytical activities.

Going forward, comprehensive statistical data and recurring publications, including the Monthly Reports (older than six months), Quarterly Bulletin of Cocoa Statistics and other statistical information (production, grindingds and others) will be made available through an annual subscription. This approach will enable the ICCO to maintain the quality, continuity and independence of its work, while continuing to deliver high-value information services to Members and stakeholders.

Stakeholders wishing to access the ICCO’s statistical information and publications on a regular basis are invited to submit this form to statistics.section@icco.org.

Quarterly Bulletin of Cocoa Statistics – English

The Quarterly Bulletin of Cocoa Statistics provides reliable data and long-term trends on the cocoa economy from 1960 onwards covering most cocoa producing and importing countries.

It is published in February, May, August and November, and contains the following:

  • Review and developments in the world cocoa market.
  • Charts on cocoa bean production, grindings, stocks and prices of cocoa beans.
  • World production and grindings of cocoa beans by region and country.
  • ICCO composite cocoa bean prices in SDRs and US dollars.
  • Cocoa bean prices on the London and New York futures markets.
  • Trade in cocoa beans, cocoa products and chocolate by country of origin and destination of exports for major importing and exporting countries.

If you need more information, please email us to: statistics.section@icco.org

Monthly Review of the Market

The Monthly Review of the cocoa market reports on the latest developments on the international cocoa futures markets (i.e. ICE Futures Europe and ICE Futures U.S.). The document is graphically illustrated and provides analysis of front-month cocoa futures contract prices denominated in U.S. dollars on both sides of the Atlantic, exchange-licensed warehouse stocks and gradings, butter and powder prices, production, grindings, and other key factors influencing the cocoa trade.

A sample of a Monthly Report is available below for reference. Access to all monthly issues published throughout the year is provided through an annual subscription.
